# Community

Everyone is welcome. We would like to help you create and register your own
OPTIMADE API implementation, and we want to hear your feedback on the
specification.

## Where to ask

| Channel | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **`dev[at]optimade.org`** | **First contact with the core developers — start here** |
| Slack | Day-to-day conversation once you are involved in the project. We do not publish an invite link, because they expire silently; ask at the address above and we will add you |
| [matsci.org forum](https://matsci.org/c/optimade/){:target="_blank"} | General questions on scope and functionality, open to the whole community |
| [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Materials-Consortia/OPTIMADE/issues){:target="_blank"} | Technical comments and proposals on the API itself |

All of our work is openly available under the
[Materials-Consortia](https://github.com/Materials-Consortia/){:target="_blank"}
organization on GitHub, and we welcome all contributions.

## Meetings

We meet online roughly once a month, on
[Jitsi](https://meet.jit.si/OPTIMADE){:target="_blank"} (room code `OPTIMADE`).
There is no fixed day: dates are announced on the mailing list, so write to
`dev[at]optimade.org` to be told about the next one. Anyone may join, and there
are no public minutes.

## Workshops

With the support of [CECAM](https://www.cecam.org){:target="_blank"} we hold an
annual workshop where the specification is discussed and developed. Past and
upcoming workshops are listed on the [events]({{ '/events' | relative_url }})
page.
