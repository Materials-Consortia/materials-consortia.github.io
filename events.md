# Events

The OPTIMADE API is developed at an annual workshop, held since 2016 and
supported by [CECAM](https://www.cecam.org){:target="_blank"} since 2018.
Anyone working on materials databases is welcome; the
[community]({{ '/community' | relative_url }}) page explains how to get in
touch, and how to join the monthly online meetings held between workshops.
